Simple Process

Get Clean in 4 Easy Steps

No appointments, no staff — just drive in, select your package, and start washing. It's that easy.

  1. 1

    Drive Into Any Open Bay

    Pull into any available wash bay or vacuum station. No booking required — just check our live bay status and go.

  2. 2

    Choose Your Wash Package

    Select Basic, Deluxe, or Ceramic Pro at the bay panel. Insert coins, bills, or tap your card — we accept all payment methods.

  3. 3

    Wash at Your Own Pace

    Use the high-pressure wand, foam brush, and all the tools provided. Need more time? Simply add more coins — the timer keeps going.

  4. 4

    Vacuum & Add-Ons

    Finish up with our high-powered vacuum stations to clean the interior. Grab air fresheners, microfiber towels, or wax from the vending station.
