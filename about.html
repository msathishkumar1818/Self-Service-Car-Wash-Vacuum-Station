Our Story

We Believe Every Car Deserves a Spotless Clean

SparkWash was founded on a simple idea — that getting a professional-quality car wash shouldn't require an appointment, a long wait, or a premium price tag. We built the infrastructure so you could do it yourself, better.

What Drives Us

Our Mission & Core Values

We exist to make premium car care accessible, affordable, and effortless for every driver — wherever they are, whenever they need it.

01

Accessibility First

Every SparkWash location is open 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. No appointments, no staff — just drive in and start washing whenever suits you.

02

Commercial Quality

We install the same pressure systems and foam equipment used by professional detailing shops — and put them in your hands at self-service prices.

03

Constant Maintenance

Every station is inspected and serviced on a weekly schedule. We won't let broken equipment ruin your wash day — and if it does, we make it right.

Our Journey

From One Bay to a Network

  1. 2014
    The First Bay Opens

    SparkWash opened its very first self-service wash bay in Cleantown, CA with just one bay and two vacuum stations. Within 3 months, it was operating at full capacity daily.

    1
  2. 2
    2016
    First Expansion — 3 New Locations

    Demand was undeniable. We opened 3 new stations in the greater metro area, each with improved equipment and a wider range of wash packages.

  3. 2019
    Contactless Payment Rolled Out

    We upgraded every bay to accept credit cards, debit cards, and contactless payments — making SparkWash the most accessible car wash network in the region.

    3
  4. 4
    2022
    Franchise Programme Launched

    We opened the SparkWash franchise programme, allowing independent operators to bring the SparkWash brand to new regions with full equipment and operational support.

  5. 2026
    12 Locations & Growing

    Today SparkWash operates 12 fully-equipped locations with 6 more in development. Our goal: making premium self-service washing available in every major metro area.

    5
